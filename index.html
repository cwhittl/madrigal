Madrigal

A native AI chat app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Bring your own keys, or run models right on your device.

Your providers

OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Cloudflare Workers AI, or any OpenAI-compatible server, using your own API keys.

On-device models

Chat with Apple Intelligence, or download open models such as Qwen3, Gemma 3 and Llama 3.2 and run them with MLX.

Projects

Group chats with shared instructions and knowledge files, so every chat in a project starts with the right context.

Agents

Save a model with its own instructions and settings, and pick it like any other model.

Private by design

No accounts, no analytics, no servers of ours. Keys stay in your Keychain, and chats sync through your own iCloud.

Native everywhere

SwiftUI on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with Markdown, code blocks, tables, search and export.
