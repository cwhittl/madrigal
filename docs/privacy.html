Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

Madrigal has no servers of its own and collects no data. It sends your requests straight to the AI providers you add, using your own keys, or runs models on your device.

In short: the developer never receives your chats, your keys, or any information about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Data leaves your device only to reach the services listed below, and only when you use the feature that needs them.

What the developer collects

Nothing. Madrigal contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and it never contacts a server run by the developer. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash logs under Apple's own privacy policy.

Where your data goes

Each service below is contacted directly from your device, and only when you use the feature described. Each service's own privacy policy governs what it does with the data it receives.

Brave Search API The search query, your Brave API key, and your IP address. Only if you choose Brave as the search service.
Your SearXNG server The search query and your IP address. Your server passes the query on to the search engines it uses. Only if you choose SearXNG and enter your server's address.
Websites in search results An ordinary page request, which includes your IP address. After a web search, the top few result pages are opened so the model can answer from them.
Open-Meteo A place name, and your IP address. When a web search asks about the weather somewhere, to fetch a forecast.
Your iCloud account Your chats, projects, agents and provider settings (never API keys) in your private iCloud database. API keys in your iCloud Keychain. To sync between your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer has no access to your private iCloud data.

What stays on your device

  • API keys and secrets are stored in the Keychain, and synced only through your own iCloud Keychain. They are never written to iCloud records or sent anywhere except the service they belong to.
  • Downloaded models stay on the device that downloaded them, and are excluded from backups.
  • Custom instructions and appearance settings are stored on each device.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete All Data removes every chat, project, agent and provider, along with their saved keys, from all devices signed in to your iCloud account. Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Data you already sent to an AI provider or search service is governed by that service's policy; contact them to delete it.

Children

Madrigal is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. The app's "Your Data" screen (Settings → Privacy) always describes the current behavior.
